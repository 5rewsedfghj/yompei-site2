NON-NEGOTIABLE

We never fabricate data or confidence

Everywhere the system doesn't know something, it says so. That's the whole reason it's usable for real compliance work.

Unknown not Active

If no source confirms status, it stays Unknown and becomes its own finding. No optimistic defaults.

Honest empty states
